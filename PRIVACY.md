# Privacy

FocusMark is a local focus tool. Its Accessibility service needs permission to detect selected foreground apps and can inspect visible labels in supported apps and browsers to identify feeds and enforce configured rules. It stores settings, schedules, counters and timing checkpoints on the device. According to the current source, raw screen text, web addresses and browsing history are not retained or sent to the development API.

Feed recognition requires consent inside the app. After consent, counts and the combined allowance continue even when the badge is hidden. Android lets you revoke Accessibility in system settings; this also stops enforcement. The bundled API is a development scaffold, not an active account or cloud service. Review this document again before distribution or any Play Store submission.
